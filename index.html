Your setup

Each model downloads separately. Base is the starting point; Small uses more storage and memory. Speed and accuracy depend on your phone.

One entry per line: preferred spelling | misheard phrase. Suggestions need your approval. Entries without a phrase are reference vocabulary, not model training.

No automatic medical rewriting. Check medications, doses, measurements, and negations before use.

Privacy & offline use

Initial downloads contact Hugging Face for model files. Speech processing runs locally with no audio or transcript uploads. The draft is not saved to storage. Copy places text on the system clipboard, whose history or syncing is controlled by your device. Model files and vocabulary stay in browser storage, which iOS can remove. Keep the app open while recording; switching apps stops capture. This app has not been validated as a clinical documentation system.
