Privacy & offline use

Initial downloads contact Hugging Face for model files. Speech processing runs locally with no audio or transcript uploads. The draft is held in memory. Choosing “Update & keep draft” temporarily saves it in this tab and removes that saved copy after reloading. Exported backups can include text only when you select that option. Copy places text on the system clipboard, whose history or syncing is controlled by your device. Model files and vocabulary stay in browser storage, which iOS can remove. Keep the app open while recording; switching apps stops capture. This app has not been validated as a clinical documentation system.
